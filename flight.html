Travel Tips

Before You Fly

A few simple tips can make your airport experience faster and more comfortable.

01
Arrive Early

Arrive at the airport at least two to three hours before your international flight.

02
Check Your Documents

Make sure your passport, visa and travel documents are ready before departure.

03
Check Your Baggage

Review your airline's baggage allowance and restrictions before packing.

04
Monitor Your Gate

Keep an eye on airport displays for any changes to your boarding gate.
